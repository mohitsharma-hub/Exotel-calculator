💡 Benchmarking Notes & Observations

▼
ECC:
• Type 1 SMB (≤20 agents): typically 8–14 PS days, total 26–34 days
• Type 2 Medium (20–80 agents): typically 14–22 PS days
• Type 4 ME/MNC (100+ agents, complex integrations): 28–54 PS days, total can exceed 100 days
• Customization (miniCRM, node flows, Konnect) significantly increases PE days
• VOC survey adds 5 PS days — common in ME/MNC deals
• PBX integration is 0 PS days (license only) but adds PE days

Chatbot:
• Simple FAQ + 1 channel: ~27 dev days, Grand Total ~38
• Complex multi-flow (6+ flows, 18 sub-flows, APIs): 54 dev days, Grand Total ~80
• Each major flow ≈ 1.5 dev days; sub flow ≈ 1 dev day
• Arabic/additional language adds 3 dev days each
• QA = 20% of Dev; PM = 20% of (Dev+QA) — confirmed from actual sheets
• On-premise deployment = 10 dev days/setup (significantly higher than cloud's 2 days)

General:
• DR/HA setup adds PE days (not tracked in PS calculator — add as custom item)
• Voicelog Archiver = license only, 0 PS days
• Report Scheduler = out-of-box, 0 PS days
